The current public surface is organized into inputs, readouts, and surfaces. The first block catalog focuses on those families, while a few additional controls remain documented here until they get their own catalog slot.

## Inputs

- [Dial](/view/dial): rotary input with metallic foil response
- [Slider](/view/slider): linear fader with recessed travel and machined thumb
- [Toggle](/view/toggle): heavy rocker control with optional LED states
- [Square Button](/view/square-button): momentary square plunger with deep 3D travel
- [Square Toggle](/view/square-toggle): latching square control with optional LED feedback
- [Switch](/view/switch): cylindrical track switch with rolling thumb behavior
- [Wheel Select](/view/wheel-select): stepped trim wheel for option navigation
- [Wheel Number](/view/wheel-number): numeric trim wheel for precise scrubbing

Browse the whole family in [Blocks / Inputs](/blocks/inputs).

## Readouts

- [Gauge](/view/gauge): circular LCD monitor with dial-face styling
- [Meter](/view/meter): stereo output monitor with analog ballistics
- [Indicator](/view/indicator): jewel lamp with bloom and bezel variants

Browse the whole family in [Blocks / Readouts](/blocks/readouts).

## Surfaces

- [Panel](/view/panel): rack-style housing for dense control groupings
- [Rocker Thumb Surface](/view/rocker-thumb-surface): reusable thumb shell used by sliders and switches

Browse the whole family in [Blocks / Surfaces](/blocks/surfaces).

## Registry Endpoints

All of the controls above are available through the package and the registry, including the square controls:

- [`/r/square-button.json`](/r/square-button.json)
- [`/r/square-toggle.json`](/r/square-toggle.json)
